701x, Inc. is a Fargo, North Dakota agricultural technology company building an end-to-end technology stack for beef cattle producers: solar-powered GPS smart ear tags (xTpro), wireless Bluetooth smart tags (xTlite), base stations and water sensors, paired with the Autonomous Rancher herd-management software and mobile apps. The platform keeps a digital calving book, animal inventory, interactive pasture map, health and activity alerts, virtual fencing, DNA and EPD records, medication and treatment logs, and two-way registration/performance sync with beef breed associations (DigitalBeef). 701x operates a first-party REST API at api.701x.com — a live, publicly readable Swagger/OpenAPI 3.0.1 contract with roughly 1,391 operations across animals, tags, farms, companies, carts, subscriptions and telemetry — secured by its own OpenID Connect identity server at login.701x.com.

**Areas:** Company · Agriculture · AgTech · Livestock · Cattle Management · Ranch Management · Animal Health · IoT · GPS Tracking · Traceability · Beef Production

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for 701x: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
